## Goal

A planner opens the latest MRP run's daily report on the MRP page: a list of the run's material-location pairs with their lowest projected balance and first shortage, filtered by material and location. Once the filters leave one pair, its 90 days show with nine columns. `GET …/mrp/runs/latest` takes the same filters and returns the same figures, for both roles and in a read-only tenant.

## Scope

As the spec's Implementation Plan says:
- `core`: `summarizePlan`, the validators, `MrpRunService.latest` (filters, filtered total, names, active flags, summary), and `current()` removed;
- `app`: `localeQuantity`, the shared material and location filter form, `MrpReport`, the page, EN/PL copy and the help topic;
- plan and timing tests;
- integration tests;
- docs.

**Non-goals:** the weekly view (E18-S02), explanations and at-risk orders (E18-S03), proposals (E16), a shortage filter (spec Q2), locale formatting on other screens (#304), and a new route (spec Q3).
