## Spec sections you implement (by heading)

- **Components**: `StatusBadge` fed by the tone map in every document status cell and in detail headers (`meta`); `Callout` for the migrated boxes and for success/notice messages in your dialogs; `PageHeader`; `Button` labels and placement; `DataTable` `kind`/`priority`/`rowTone`.
- **Tokens and colour → Hardcoded colours to migrate**: `app/src/components/goods-receipts/receipt-dialog.tsx:71` (`text-amber-700 dark:text-amber-400` lines → `<Callout tone="warning">` holding a `<ul>`) and `app/src/components/production-orders/completion-form.tsx:150` (`border-amber-500 bg-amber-50 dark:bg-amber-950` + `role="alert"` → `<Callout tone="warning" role="alert">`, the accept checkbox stays inside).
- **Layout and the page frame → `DataTable`**: every table in scope gets `kind` per column (`id`, `text`, `longText`, `quantity`, `date`, `status`, `actions`) and `priority` (1 essential, 2 hidden below `md`, 3 hidden below `lg`; `status` is always 1); the ID column first so it is the sticky one.
- **Typography and numbers**: quantities right-aligned with `tabular-nums` (the `quantity` kind), the unit in a stable column or label, signed movements with their sign; IDs in `font-mono` (the `id` kind); empty values `—` with `aria-label="none"`; weights 400/500/600 (`font-bold` → `font-semibold`).
- **Spacing, radius and surfaces**: `0.5`/`1.5` in `packages/app` → `1` or `2`; `rounded-lg` on in-page boxes → `rounded-md` (callouts) or `rounded-lg` only for cards; no card inside a card; tables on the page background with row dividers, not in a Card.
- **States and copy**: Empty states say what is missing and the next step (a create action for a writer, a hint for a reader or a read-only tenant), filtered-empty offers "Clear filters" where a filter exists; success messages after a posted receipt, issue, completion or shipment are `Callout tone="success" role="status"`, no duplicate success panels; the Reversal confirmation names the exact record, consequence and reason.
- **Edge Cases → Two statuses look alike**: Open (`CircleDot`) and Cancelled (`CircleX`) are both neutral: the icon and the label separate them; check a mixed list in both themes.
