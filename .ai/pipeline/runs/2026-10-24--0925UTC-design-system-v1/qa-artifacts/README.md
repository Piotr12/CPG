# Close QA captures

A selection of the lead's close QA on the realistic dataset: one light English 1440 px and one dark Polish 320 px capture per acceptance screen, plus the keyboard, dialog, not-found and refused-sign-in checks. `metrics.json` and `metrics-shortage.json` record every one of the 124 captures taken (no page overflow, one `h1`, clipping only on the icon rail's hidden labels at 768 px); the full set was attached to the pull request's QA comment as a selection and kept out of the repository for size.
