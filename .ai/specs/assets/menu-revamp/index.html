🦣 CPG menu revamp: five clickable mockups

Five different answers to one brief (BRIEF.md). They cover what is on main, #23 (locations, customers, suppliers) and #25 (read-only tenants), which are in progress, and the later epics: planning parameters and safety stock (#24), BOMs (#26), spreadsheet import (#27), and the slice 2/3 operations, MRP and reports. Static HTML: no build, no network.

Test/Prod is a switch, never a menu entry Master data is one menu tree Read-only tenant: 🔒, banner, disabled writes Admin vs normal user Later slices shown greyed (toggleable) Mobile at 390 px

In every mockup, the dashed Mockup controls box in the bottom-right corner switches role, read-only and later slices. About this concept explains the idea.

01Sidebar tree

The familiar ERP layout. A full-width TEST | PROD switch heads the sidebar. Everything tenant-scoped sits under it: Overview, the master-data tree (expanded), greyed later groups, Audit. Everything global is pinned below a "Shared by Test and Prod" caption. Collapses to an icon rail; a drawer on mobile.

  • Every existing screen visible and 1 click away
  • The split between tenant and global screens is visible at a glance
  • Always costs 264 px of width; tight on height once the later slices exist
  • On mobile everything is behind the Menu button

02Top bar + mega-menu

Today's top bar, evolved. Six plain words (Master data · Operations · Planning · Reports · Audit · Admin) each open a panel that describes every screen in one line. A tab row inside each section moves between sibling screens. The TEST ◐ PROD pill is on the right, and the bar's bottom edge takes the tenant colour.

  • Smallest change from today; tables keep the full width
  • The descriptions double as a map of the product
  • Most screens are 2 clicks away; the map is hidden until a panel opens
  • Polish labels crowd the bar below ~880 px

03Launcher + command palette

Minimal chrome. The Overview is the menu: tiles with record counts and recent changes. ⌘K searches screens, records (00000003, "flint") and actions ("switch to prod", "make Prod read-only"). Recent and pinned screens sit as chips under the bar; master-data pages keep a mini-tree.

  • The fastest for power users; finds a record by ID or name
  • Scales to ~35 screens without a bigger menu
  • The full menu is not on screen while you work
  • The palette has to be discovered
Open mockup 03 →

04Process flow

The menu teaches MRP. A ribbon of stages runs ① Master data → ② Demand → ③ Plan → ④ Supply → ⑤ Stock → ⑥ Ship, then a separate Look back stage. The open stage's screens are in a left panel; for Master data that panel is the one tree. A sliding toggle says "You are in TEST — playground". The Overview is a timeline with the next sensible step.

  • Best fit for the "a caveman can follow the planning logic" vision
  • Slices 2 and 3 slot into stages that are already there
  • Two layers cost ~100 px of height; the ribbon gets busy
  • In slice 1 only Master data and Look back work
Open mockup 04 →

05Icon rail + panel

Linear/VS Code style. A 64 px icon column is tinted in the tenant colour (amber Test, black/red Prod, grey on shared pages), with a T/P switch at its top. Clicking an area opens a panel with its tree, record counts and a filter box. The panel can be pinned or collapsed. On mobile, bottom tabs: Home · Master data · Operations · Audit · More.

  • Hardest to mistake Prod for Test; colour in peripheral vision
  • The best mobile story (bottom tabs)
  • Rail + pinned panel take ~300 px of width
  • Small icon labels; Polish wraps

How to review

  1. Open each mockup from the cards above. Use Back in the browser; every screen has its own URL.
  2. Try the same five tasks in each:
    1. open a material and its unit conversions;
    2. go to Locations, then Suppliers;
    3. flip Test → Prod and check that you stay on the same screen;
    4. as the admin, make Prod read-only on the Tenants page and look at the lock, the banner and the disabled buttons;
    5. change the language or the theme.
  3. Switch Role to Normal user in the mockup controls: the admin entries disappear and master data becomes read-only.
  4. Narrow the window, or use DevTools' device toolbar (⌥⌘I, then ⇧⌘M, iPhone): every mockup has a 390 px layout.
  5. Reply with a mix ("02's bar + 01's tree + 03's palette" is a valid answer). The chosen combination becomes the spec for the menu rebuild.
