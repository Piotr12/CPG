Signing in

Screen: Sign in (/login)

Enter your username and password. The built-in administrator signs in as admin with the password set when CPG was installed.

If the details are wrong you see “Invalid username or password.” — CPG never says which part was wrong. After several failed attempts sign-in is paused for 15 minutes.

Cave control

Screen: Cave control (/admin)

The Database card shows where CPG keeps its data and whether every migration has been applied. Use Sign out in the header when you are done.
