# D08 — Statistical unit, demand review screen, and forecast consumption by calendar month

- Date, owner: 2026-10-25, Piotr12. The owner asked for a demand review screen and a statistical unit, chose among the options on #240 and #239, and accepted the agent's recommendations there.
- Decision 1, statistical unit: one statistical unit `SU`, the same in every tenant, used only in reports. Each product's factor is a material conversion. Every entered quantity stays in physical units. Spec: `.ai/specs/implemented/2026-10-25--0913UTC-statistical-unit.md`.
- Decision 2, demand review: a Demand review screen and its read API show shipped history and demand still to ship, in day, week or month buckets. When more than one product is selected, quantities are reported in SU only. Spec: `.ai/specs/2026-10-25--0913UTC-demand-review.md`.
- Decision 3, forecast consumption: a forecast is consumed by the customer-order lines of the same material and location whose required date falls in the forecast's calendar month. Consumption is allocated to that month's forecasts in date order. The consuming quantity is `quantity − abandoned`, so closing early and cancelling release what never shipped, and fully shipped lines keep consuming. A residual dated before today in the current month counts as due today. Forecasts of months that have ended expire.
- Why: D04 froze the four research documents as the baseline. The screen and SU are new owner requirements. The realistic dataset, like monthly planning in general, dates forecasts once a month. Under DEM-007's same-date isolation, orders later in the month never consume that forecast, so demand is counted twice: the #240 prototype showed November at about 6 pallets for a product that ships about 3 a month. MRP (#14) would plan the same overstatement.
- Supersedes: DEM-007's "isolated by original date". Forecasts stay isolated by material, location (DEM-005/006) and **calendar month**. This Markdown entry is the normative erratum to the historical PDF export and `CPG-acceptance.md` (DEM-007 carries a note). No other baseline requirement changes. DEM-002…013 still hold as restated in the demand review spec.
