## 📝 TLDR

Demand beyond firm orders could not be expressed, so the slice 3 MRP run would have nothing to net orders against. This story adds the **forecast**: the quantity of a material expected at a location on a day, with no customer. It is kept in the UI (a **Forecasts** screen under a new **Sales** group of the sidebar) and the API, listed per day and filterable by material and location. Consumption by orders, the residual forecast and overdue demand (DEM-002…013) are the MRP run's, not this story's.

## Data model

`forecast` (tenant-owned): `id`, `tenant_code`, `material_id`, `location_id`, `date` (ISO text), `quantity` (six-decimal text), `version`; unique on material, location and date. Migration `Migration20261020164210_forecasts`, with an up/down test.

## API (protected contract, `BACKWARD_COMPATIBILITY.md` §4)

`GET`, `POST /api/tenants/{tenant}/forecasts`; `PATCH`, `DELETE /api/tenants/{tenant}/forecasts/{material}/{location}/{date}`.

## Out of scope

Customers on forecasts, consumption by orders, the residual forecast, overdue demand and the MRP run (DEM-002…013, SAL-014…017, slice 3); importing forecasts; moving a forecast to another day.
